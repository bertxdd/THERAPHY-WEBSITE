## How to Set Up Email Functionality

### Step 1: Create a Free EmailJS Account
1. Go to https://www.emailjs.com/
2. Sign up for a free account
3. Verify your email

### Step 2: Set Up Your Email Service
1. In EmailJS dashboard, go to "Email Services"
2. Click "Add New Service"
3. Select "Gmail" (or your preferred provider)
4. Follow the authentication steps to connect your Gmail account
5. **Note the Service ID** (you'll need this)

### Step 3: Create an Email Template
1. Go to "Email Templates" in EmailJS dashboard
2. Click "Create New Template"
3. Use these settings:
   - **To Email:** {{to_email}}
   - **From Name:** TherapyHub
   - **Subject:** Your Therapy Appointment Confirmation - {{appointment_date}}
   - **Template Body:**
     ```
     Hello {{user_name}},

     Your therapy appointment has been successfully booked!

     APPOINTMENT DETAILS:
     ==================
     Name: {{user_name}}
     Email: {{user_email}}
     Phone: {{user_phone}}
     Therapy Type: {{therapy_type}}
     Date: {{appointment_date}}
     Time: {{appointment_time}}
     Notes: {{user_message}}

     If you need to reschedule or have any questions, please contact us.

     Best regards,
     TherapyHub Team
     ```
4. **Note the Template ID** (you'll need this)

### Step 4: Get Your Public Key
1. Go to "Account" → "API Keys" in EmailJS dashboard
2. Copy your **Public Key**

### Step 5: Update script.js
1. Open `script.js`
2. Find the line: `emailjs.init("YOUR_PUBLIC_KEY_HERE");`
3. Replace `YOUR_PUBLIC_KEY_HERE` with your actual Public Key

4. Find the line: `emailjs.send("YOUR_SERVICE_ID", "YOUR_TEMPLATE_ID", emailParams);`
5. Replace:
   - `YOUR_SERVICE_ID` with your Service ID
   - `YOUR_TEMPLATE_ID` with your Template ID

### Example (after replacement):
```javascript
emailjs.init("1a2b3c4d5e6f7g8h9i");
...
emailjs.send("gmail_1a2b3c", "template_xyz123", emailParams);
```

## Features Explained

### Calendar
- Shows current month by default
- Navigate between months with arrow buttons
- Automatically disables past dates
- Click any available date to select it
- Selected date appears in the form

### Form Fields
- **Full Name** - Required
- **Email** - Required, used for confirmation
- **Phone** - Required
- **Therapy Type** - Dropdown (Individual, Couples, Family, Group)
- **Time** - 9 options from 9 AM to 5 PM
- **Additional Notes** - Optional special requirements

### Sections
1. **Home** - Hero section with CTA
2. **Services** - 4 service offerings
3. **About Us** - Company info and features
4. **Contact** - Contact details
5. **Booking Form** - Main appointment form with calendar

## Troubleshooting

### Email not sending?
- Check that Public Key, Service ID, and Template ID are correct
- Verify Gmail account is properly authenticated in EmailJS
- Check browser console (F12) for error messages
- Ensure form is filled completely

### Calendar not showing?
- Make sure script.js is loaded (check console for errors)
- Verify index.html has correct path to script.js

### Form validation issues?
- Ensure all required fields have values before submitting
- Check that a date is selected from the calendar
